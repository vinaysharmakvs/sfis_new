LONG-RANGE ILLUSTRATION

A 10-year growth plan.

Starts with 150 students in Year 1, then adds 150 students each year. Per-student fees rise 5% yearly and operating costs rise 2% yearly. Bus tax and insurance scale with the modeled bus count (one bus per 50 students). There is no construction-and-bus reserve through 300 students. Above 300 students, the reserve is ₹30 lakh for every additional 150, rising 2% yearly. Each investor contributes only their 4% or 8.5% share of that reserve; school earnings cover the balance before investor distributions are calculated.
